## Preserved brand and partner assets

The NexAge logo, brand mark, favicon and local partner logo files were preserved. The TONE partner logo is referenced from `https://www.tonetool.co.jp/assets/img/common/logo_white.svg`; its current availability and permission for this use were not re-verified during this image audit.

Other legacy image files remain in `public/images/` for compatibility and historical reference. Their provenance is not inferred from their filenames. For known catalog entries, the canonical local image in `src/data.ts` takes precedence over older API `image_path` values, which prevents stale database paths from reintroducing repeated or mismatched images. Newly added catalog entries can still use their API-provided image path.
